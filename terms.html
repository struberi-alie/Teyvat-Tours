Terms and Conditions

Please read these terms before requesting a booking. They explain how trip requests, payments, changes, and travel arrangements are handled.

1. Booking requests and confirmation

Submitting a booking request does not guarantee a reservation. Your trip is confirmed only after Teyvat Tours verifies availability and provides written confirmation of the itinerary, travel dates, traveler count, and agreed payment arrangements.

2. Traveler information

Please provide accurate names, contact details, and travel preferences. Review your details before submitting a request and notify the travel team if anything changes. A parent or legal guardian must arrange travel for minors.

3. Package prices and inclusions

Displayed prices are starting rates per person unless stated otherwise. The final quotation identifies the applicable price, duration, accommodation, transport, activities, and inclusions. Do not assume that meals, transfers, admission fees, or other services are included unless they are listed in your confirmed package.

4. Payment arrangements

Available payment options may include GCash, credit or debit card, and bank transfer. The travel team will provide the payment schedule and verified instructions before payment is due. Selecting a payment method on the booking form does not authorize a charge. Keep a copy of your payment confirmation and never send passwords, PINs, or one-time security codes through the inquiry form.

5. Changes, cancellations, and refunds

Contact the travel team as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel your trip. Availability, deadlines, supplier conditions, and any applicable fees or refund rules will be provided with your quotation before you commit to a booking. Refund eligibility and processing details depend on those agreed conditions and any applicable consumer rights.

6. Itinerary changes and disruptions

Weather, transport interruptions, site closures, and safety concerns may affect the planned itinerary. If a change is necessary, the travel team will explain the available alternatives and any price changes. Material changes should be discussed with the traveler before proceeding whenever practicable.

7. Travel requirements and special requests

Travelers are responsible for checking the documents and entry requirements relevant to their itinerary. Tell the travel team about accessibility needs, dietary preferences, or other arrangements before booking. Special requests are subject to availability and are confirmed only when the team explicitly accepts them.

8. Safety and traveler conduct

Follow local rules, site instructions, and your guide’s safety directions. Respect other travelers, local communities, wildlife, and cultural sites. Keep personal belongings secure and report concerns promptly so the travel team can assist.

9. Use of booking information

The booking process uses the information you provide to respond to your request and arrange the agreed travel services. Share only the details needed for your trip. You may contact the travel team to correct booking information or ask how your details are handled.

10. Questions and acceptance

Read your quotation and confirmed package details alongside these terms. If anything is unclear, ask the travel team before agreeing or paying. Checking the agreement box confirms that you have read these terms and understand that the final trip remains subject to availability and written confirmation.
