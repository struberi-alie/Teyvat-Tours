Teyvat Tours

Privacy Information

OUR COMMITMENT TO YOUR PRIVACY

Teyvat Tours respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you browse our website, contact our team, or book a tour with us.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

When you submit an inquiry or request a booking, we may collect your name, email address, contact number, preferred destination, travel dates, number of travelers, and booking preferences. Additional details may be requested when necessary to arrange accommodations, transportation, or other travel services.

If you choose to share accessibility needs, dietary requirements, or other sensitive details, please provide only the information needed to arrange your requested assistance.

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use your information to respond to inquiries, prepare quotations, arrange requested travel services, manage bookings, and communicate important updates about your trip. We may also use relevant records to resolve concerns, prevent misuse of our services, and meet applicable obligations.

WHEN WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION

We may share necessary booking details with hotels, transportation providers, tour guides, and other service partners involved in your itinerary. The information shared is limited to what is needed to provide the requested service.

We do not sell your personal information. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or threats to safety.

PAYMENT INFORMATION

Payment instructions are provided through our designated booking channels. Depending on your chosen payment method, payment providers may process information under their own privacy policies. We may retain transaction references and payment confirmations for booking verification and accounting purposes.

Do not send card security codes, account passwords, PINs, or one-time passwords through our inquiry forms or messages.

WEBSITE AND TECHNICAL INFORMATION

When you visit our website, our hosting provider may record technical information such as your IP address, browser type, requested pages, and access times to support website operation and security. Your browser may also store cached files and information saved through its autofill settings.

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts. These requests may share technical information with Google, whose handling of that information is covered by its own privacy policy.

INFORMATION SECURITY AND RETENTION

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. Access is limited to people who need the information to carry out their responsibilities. However, no online transmission or storage method can guarantee complete security.

We retain information only for as long as necessary to manage your booking, address concerns, and meet applicable legal or accounting requirements. Information that is no longer needed is securely deleted or anonymized.

YOUR PRIVACY CHOICES

You may contact us to ask about your personal information, request access or corrections, or request its deletion where applicable. You may also withdraw consent for uses that depend on your consent. Some requests may affect our ability to provide a service or may be subject to recordkeeping requirements.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Please provide only the details necessary to help us locate the relevant inquiry or booking.

CHILDREN AND OTHER TRAVELERS

A parent or legal guardian should arrange bookings for minors. If you provide information on behalf of other travelers, please make sure you are authorized to do so and that they are aware of this Privacy Notice.

CHANGES TO THIS NOTICE

We may update this Privacy Notice when our services or information-handling practices change. The latest version will be available on this page, with significant changes communicated when appropriate.

Privacy Questions or Concerns?

Visit our inquiry page and select “Others” to submit a privacy question or request. Please avoid including sensitive documents in your initial message.
