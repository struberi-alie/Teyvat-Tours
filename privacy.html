Privacy Information

Information about the current website prototype.

Booking and inquiry forms

The current forms demonstrate the travel booking interface. They are not connected to a reservation database, payment service, or email delivery system. Use sample information when trying the prototype.

Information entered in your browser

Your browser may remember fields through its own autofill or history features. Avoid entering payment card details, passwords, or security codes. The payment choices on the form do not collect card credentials.

Fonts and hosting

Some pages can load fonts from Google Fonts when an internet connection is available. Hosting and external resource providers may receive ordinary request information such as an IP address when their resources are loaded.

Before live bookings begin

This information describes the current prototype. A live booking service will need an updated notice explaining its actual data collection, use, retention, contact details, and privacy request process.
